# Hi, I'm Huashuo Lei (雷化硕)

I'm an MPhil student in Robotics and Autonomous Systems at **The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)**. I received my bachelor's degree in Robotics Engineering from **Harbin Institute of Technology, Weihai**. My research focuses on **embodied AI, robot learning, vision-language-action (VLA) models, and memory-dependent long-horizon robotic manipulation**, with an interest in world/action models. I'm working on **[RoboMemArena](https://github.com/OpenHelix-Team/RoboMemArena)**, a benchmark for memory-dependent robotic manipulation, and the **PrediMem** method.
